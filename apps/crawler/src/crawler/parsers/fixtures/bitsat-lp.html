About BITSAT

The Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT) is the official entrance examination for admission to First Degree programmes at BITS Pilani. It assesses conceptual clarity, analytical reasoning, and problem solving ability, and serves as the sole gateway to B.E., M.Sc., and B.Pharm. programmes across BITS Campuses. The process is fully merit based and computer driven, with no management quota or alternate entry route.
